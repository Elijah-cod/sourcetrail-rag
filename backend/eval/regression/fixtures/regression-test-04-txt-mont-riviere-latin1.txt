MONT RIVIÈRE AERIAL STATION LOG

Revision: 4.9
Supervisor: Maëlle Girard
Inspection date: 7 August 2049
Station code: MR-AIR-518

FLIGHT SCHEDULE

The survey balloon Éclat launches at 05:40 every Wednesday and Sunday from Platform Côte-Nord. A standard ascent lasts 65 minutes. The launch authorization phrase is Brume Claire.

PRELAUNCH CHECKLIST

1. Confirm envelope pressure is 102 kilopascals.
2. Test beacon Lumière-5 for four pulses.
3. Secure sample crate Vézin-2.
4. Record the crew count in ledger Noël-14.

HELIUM ALLOCATION

The station receives 640 cubic metres of helium each month. Research flights receive 50 percent, weather patrols receive 30 percent, and the emergency reserve receives 20 percent.

Reorder helium only when the reserve falls below 96 cubic metres. A low-pressure warning by itself does not trigger a helium reorder.

AMBER WIND WARNING

When wind exceeds 58 kilometres per hour, cancel the next balloon launch, move the Éclat basket into Hangar Trois, and announce code AIGLE DORÉ on radio channel Nuage-7.

If the hangar door is open during an Amber warning, close it with key 42 before moving the basket. Do not activate the lightning mast unless lightning is also detected.

LIGHTNING RESPONSE

When lightning is detected within 18 kilometres, activate mast Foudre-3 and disconnect the balloon tether controller. This rule applies whether or not an Amber wind warning is active.

If lightning and an Amber warning occur together, first cancel the launch, then close the hangar door with key 42, move the Éclat basket into Hangar Trois, activate mast Foudre-3, disconnect the tether controller, and announce AIGLE DORÉ on Nuage-7.

COMMAND SUCCESSION

If Maëlle Girard is unavailable, engineer Lucien Rêve assumes command. If both Maëlle and Lucien are unavailable, radio operator Anaïs Bérard assumes command.

MEDICAL SUPPLIES

The emergency cabinet contains 16 thermal blankets, 10 oxygen masks, and 4 trauma kits. During a cold-exposure incident, issue thermal blankets before oxygen masks. A trauma kit is opened only after medic Zoë Martin authorizes it.
